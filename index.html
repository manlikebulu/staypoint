What can I book on StayPoint?

Hotels, apartments and guesthouses. Choose a location, property type or keyword and explore available stays.

How do I make a booking?

Search for a destination, explore available stays, review the property details and policies, then proceed through the booking process to confirm your reservation.

03

When will I be charged for my booking?

Payment is made as part of the booking process. The amount and applicable payment details are shown before you confirm your booking.

04

What happens if a hotel rejects my booking?

If a hotel rejects your booking, the payment made for that booking is fully refunded according to StayPoint's refund process.

05

Can I cancel my booking?

Cancellation depends on the booking's applicable policy. Review the cancellation terms shown for your stay before confirming your booking.
